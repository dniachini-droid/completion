# Tools — productivity features, built into the world

> Phase 2 amendment (D-016). The best parts of productivity apps, each one **part of the game**, not bolted on. Dan: "all this stuff I kind of want integrated. Not just plonked into the app."
> Names in *italics* are placeholders. The real names come from the chosen world (Phase 3, `narrative/TERMINOLOGY.md`).

_Status: draft for Dan's review, 2026-09-23._

## The rule for every tool (P16)
A tool earns its place only if (1) using it moves or reveals something in the world, and (2) it never creates a pile, a debt or a red number. If a tool can't meet both, it doesn't go in.

## 1. The focus timer → *the delve*
The 25/5 Pomodoro that already works for Dan, made into a moment in the world.
- **While it runs**, the expedition is visibly moving through the Site. It is not a number counting down. Dan can put the phone away; nothing needs watching.
- **The 5-minute break** is a short *camp* beat: a glance at what the delve turned up.
- **Each finished delve** = a **step** (same as `CORE_LOOPS.md` Part 4). It can be started on a job, on a list (below), or on nothing in particular after day complete.
- **Four in a row** (a *deep delve*) reaches somewhere a single one can't: a deeper chamber, or a find. Long focus is rewarded in kind, not with bigger numbers.
- Stopping early loses nothing. Minutes done still count towards the job's hour.
- Anti-farming is unchanged: only time earns, and it slows after about 2 extra hours of the same kind of work.

## 2. Lists → *the satchel*
For the days Dan has a list ("sort the flat before the visit").
- Add **one line or many at once** (paste a list). Group them if wanted ("flat move").
- **Never on the opening screen.** Opening still shows one next thing (P1). The satchel opens on request.
- **Working a list = a delve on the list.** Start the timer on the list and tick items as you go. The delve earns the step, so ten tiny ticks never out-earn real time (rule 10, P13). Any item can also be accepted as one of the day's main jobs.
- **Nothing piles up.** No counts, no overdue marks. Items untouched for about 3 weeks quietly move to *someday*. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is announced.
- The morning suggestion can draw from the satchel, and still leans towards what is avoided (P5).

## 3. Scheduling → *plotting a route*
Optional planning ahead, for the things that have a time ("Spanish lesson Thursday 6 pm").
- Dan can **plot** a job onto a day, or a day and time. On the map it becomes a **waypoint** on the route ahead.
- That day, the plotted job leads the morning suggestion. Reaching a waypoint Dan plotted leaves a small landmark on the map.
- **If the time passes:** the job goes quietly back to the satchel. The app may ask once, "move it?". There's no red and no "missed" (P8).
- **Light, not time-blocking.** No hour-by-hour calendar to fill in. Planning is never required to play (P1: starting beats planning).

## 4. Calendar link
- Reads Dan's Google Calendar. Appointments appear as the day's jobs **without typing anything**, and the day shrinks to fit (P10). Plotted waypoints can optionally appear in the calendar.
- **Feasibility is checked in Phase 7** (technical architecture). It is not in the first playable.

## 5. Streaks → *runs* (the non-punitive kind)
Dan likes streaks; he dislikes *punitive* ones (Phase 0). So:
- **A daily run** counts days in a row with **day complete**. A **low day counts** (P3), and a day marked **rested counts** (P12). One missed day doesn't end a run: a run ends only after **two** days in a row with no day complete.
- **Weekly runs** count weeks in a row that a weekly target is met (gym 4×, Spanish…).
- **In the world:** each day of a run lights the next *lamp* along a path. At about 7, 14, 30 and 60 days, a run reveals something only runs reveal: a **relic** for the collection (see below).
- **When a run ends, nothing is lost.** Lit lamps stay lit forever. Relics stay. The best run is kept in the Chronicle. A new run starts at the next day complete, with no "streak lost" message, just the first lamp of a new path.
- Anti-farming: runs need day complete, which is slot-based, so tiny tasks can't feed them.

## 6. The Chronicle
The record of what Dan actually did, written as the expedition's own journal.
- Each week gets a short page, written automatically from real completions: where you went, what the real week held (gym ×4, Spanish lesson, the course module), the runs, what opened.
- It is **evidence that he moved**, for low days: "look how far you've come", without being a stats dashboard. There are no charts, no percentages and no comparisons with other weeks.
- Dan never writes in it. He can add a line if he wants to (optional).
- It grows into the story's timeline of *Dan's* expedition, next to the ancient lives he is uncovering.

## Collection
Relics from runs join things found in the Site (finds on avoided jobs, deep delves) in one collection (`COLLECTIONS.md`). Each relic belongs to the world truth and can be read with the script. Nothing is bought, and nothing is random loot.

## First playable
**In:** the delve (timer), the satchel (lists), daily runs with lamps and the first relic, and a simple Chronicle page.
**Later:** deep-delve variety, weekly runs, plotting and waypoints, the calendar link (after the Phase 7 check).
