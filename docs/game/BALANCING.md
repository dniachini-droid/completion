## The idea in one paragraph
There are **two clocks**. **Time moves the Site:** every real minute moves Dan, uncapped. **The story keeps its order:** records, signs and words arrive in a fixed sequence at a steady pace, however big the week. When Dan's hours run ahead of the story, the extra goes to places, side chambers, finds and the deep route, never to a wall and never to story out of order (D-035, D-037, D-039).

## 2. The story clock
- The story is written in **story weeks**. A story week holds about **5 arrivals, 2–3 record fragments, 3–5 Key openings and 1–2 signs**; words about one a month after the first in week 2–3 (D-013).
- **At most one story week per calendar week.** A story week lasts as long as it takes to deliver its beats: a thin week stretches it into the next, and a week away pauses it (the world waits; P8). A big week can't start the next one early.
- Beats arrive **on real action**, in order: a story line on a job's return, an arrival on day complete, a sealed thing when a Key lands. Minutes move the map; they don't hurry the story.
- **Places may run ahead by up to one story week** (the deep push's rule). A record found early is seen, and reads in its turn. Signs never come early; a **partial sign** at most, and only where one is written (D-013).
- Past that, extra effort goes to the **open route** (section 5).

## 3. Keys
| Number | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Useful Keys a week (the bounded supply) | **5** | About 250 sealed things are written for the year (≈ 5 a week). Adding rhythms never raises it (D-047 invariant) |
| How they're earned | each rhythm met = 1 Key, the moment it's met, until the week's 5 are used | Dan's six rhythms meet about 5 in a full week (the tank clean is fortnightly), so an honest full week reaches the supply |
| Where a Key goes | to the next **story** count in view first, then a side count | the story's pace comes first (D-035, D-043 F6) |
| A rhythm met after the supply is used | **one find** (at most one a week) | effort is never turned away (D-039) without adding Keys |
| **The weekly floor** | a week with **at least one day complete** brings **at least 2 Keys**; the week close opens the rest | keeps the story moving through a bad week and protects the first word in week 2–3 (D-043) |
| A new rhythm or changed number | counts from its next full week | D-043 F7 |
| A rhythm far beyond its enough | pays in steps and finds, and no longer opens an extra sealed thing | the old "strongly exceeded target" opening would have broken the Key invariant (D-047) |
| Milestone Keys (great doors) | one per real milestone, confirmed with one tap, and **only for that project's great door**, which has a fixed number of parts set when the project is added | outside the weekly supply, and bounded by the door itself |

A week never shows how many Keys it has left, and a rhythm never shows a Key "owed" (UX 6).

## 5. More effort: what it always unlocks (D-011, D-035, D-039)
- **Every delve after day complete is a step.** No daily cap, no slowdown (D-044).
- **Every side chamber holds a find.** About **one in three** holds a record-bearing find (a told line or a sealed record from the side supply); the rest hold an object or a mark detail. (Answers "how often a long delve's side chamber holds an authored find".)
- **Switching jobs after a long stretch** brings a find (section 1).
- **The deep route** (a High day's deep push, or "Keep going" on any day): places up to one story week ahead, and at most **one partial sign a week**, only where one is written.
- **The open route** past each opened place is written to hold **3 Normal weeks of steps** (about 120) of passages and side chambers before a passage line repeats. Past that (extreme hours only), passage lines may repeat, side chambers keep giving finds from the region's pool, and the route still extends. Never a wall.

## 7. Memory and recap (D-035)
So a story told in small pieces stays remembered, with no homework:
- **The week close** names up to **3 things learned** that week, in one line each.
- **The first week close of each month** adds a short **"so far"**: the open questions in **3–5 lines**, in the world's words. Written with the story, never generated.
- **Before → now:** when a new sign changes an old record, the record shows its old reading faded above the new one. Surfaced **one a visit** at most, and only when Dan chooses to look (`CORE_LOOPS.md`, re-read).
- **After an absence:** "where you were" also names the **one** open question he was closest to.
- **The test** asks whether Dan remembers what he learned and whether it feels like discovery, not homework (D-035).

## 8. Checks
- **Dan's honest Normal week** (his rhythms, `PLANNER.md`): gym 4 × 1 h ≈ 9.6 steps; Spanish study 2 × 1 h ≈ 4.8; the lesson ≈ 2.4; the Course 4 days at about 2 h ≈ 19; meal prep, tank clean and one-offs ≈ 5. **About 40 steps: about 5 named places**, one story week. Keys: 5.
- **A Low week** (two days complete): about 8–10 steps, one or two places, and the floor's 2 Keys. The story week stretches; nothing is lost.
- **A huge week** (High every day, about 100 steps): the story week's places, then next week's places, then the open route, side chambers and finds. Keys: still 5.
- **Farming:** splitting work earns nothing (steps are time); more rhythms add no Keys; ten tiny jobs fill no extra slots; finds from switching need 100 minutes on one job first.
- **The year:** about 260 arrivals, about 250 sealed things (5 a week), 52 signs, 9 words: what the sealed story holds.
