# Economy

> Every currency or resource: source, sink, purpose, reason to exist, exploit risks, relation to real tasks. Fewer is better. Phase 2.

_Status: **agreed with Dan** 2026-09-23 (D-010–D-013)._

## Decision: no currencies (D-012)

The chosen loop needs **no currency at all**. Real action is turned directly into steps, arrivals and Keys (`CORE_LOOPS.md` Part 4). Nothing is bought, so nothing needs to be earned and spent.

Why:
- Every currency needs a real sink. The only candidate sinks (buying gear, buying hints, buying routes) would add a shop and a spending decision without adding anything Dan cares about.
- Currencies invite farming and hoarding, and they turn rewards into bookkeeping.
- A currency would be a number that goes up, which is exactly the kind of number the player model says Dan doesn't care about.

## What exists instead (not currencies)
| Thing | Source | Is it spent? | Why it exists | Exploit risk and guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Steps** | real time: 25 minutes = one step, in proportion; jobs without a timer by their usual length (D-037) | No: a step *is* movement | Makes every real action move the world | Time can't be split cheaply; rewards attach to slots; same-kind work slows after ~2 extra hours |
| **Keys** | weekly targets met; big milestones | Used once, on a sealed thing already seen that shows it needs a Key (D-013) | Makes the week matter without a meeting | Weekly targets are Dan's own real commitments; milestones are confirmed with one tap |
| **Signs** | Keys, some arrivals and records; *partial* signs from deep pushes (D-013) | No: permanent knowledge | Reading, re-reading and powers | Paced by authored placement, so they can't be farmed |

## Revisit when
A real need appears in use that nothing above covers. Not before (rule 12, earn complexity).
