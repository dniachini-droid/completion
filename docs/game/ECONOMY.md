# Economy

> Every currency or resource: source, sink, purpose, reason to exist, exploit risks, relation to real tasks. Fewer is better. Phase 2.

_Status: **agreed with Dan** 2026-09-23 (D-010–D-013)._

## Decision: no currencies (D-012)

The chosen loop needs **no currency at all**. Real action is turned directly into steps, arrivals and Keys (`CORE_LOOPS.md` Part 4). Nothing is bought, so nothing needs to be earned and spent.

Why:
- Every currency needs a real sink. The only candidate sinks (buying gear, buying hints, buying routes) would add a shop and a spending decision without adding anything Dan cares about.
- Currencies invite farming and hoarding, and they turn rewards into bookkeeping.
- A currency would be a number that goes up, which is exactly the kind of number the player model says Dan doesn't care about.

## What exists instead (not currencies)
| Thing | Source | Is it spent? | Why it exists | Exploit risk and guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Steps** | real time: 25 minutes = one step, in proportion; jobs without a timer by their usual length (D-037) | No: a step *is* movement | Makes every real action move the world | Time can't be split cheaply; rewards attach to slots; switching kinds after a long stretch brings a find, and staying never costs (D-044) |

Today shows the count, and a sealed thing ahead says "Needs a Key" (Dan, D-142, replacing D-043's "never a count") | Makes the week matter without a meeting | Weekly targets are Dan's own real commitments; milestones are confirmed with one tap |
| **Signs** | Keys, some arrivals and records; *partial* signs from deep pushes (D-013) | No: permanent knowledge | Reading, re-reading and powers | Paced by authored placement, so they can't be farmed |

## Invariant: more rhythms never mean more Keys (D-047)
**Adding rhythms must never increase the most Keys a period can usefully give.** Each period (a week) has a bounded Key supply set by the game, not by how many rhythms Dan has; meeting rhythms is how he earns it, and a fortnightly rhythm counts in the week it's met. Once that supply is used up, further real effort still pays in full through steps, distance, finds, side chambers and the deep route (D-037, D-039, D-044). No sealed thing, core or side, can be reached faster by splitting one commitment into several rhythms.
- **Why now:** side content is real reward, so "extra Keys only open side things" (D-043 F6) did not close the loop on its own.
- **Left to step 3** (`product/PHASE5_PLAN.md`): the size of the supply, how rhythms fill it, and the weekly floor (D-043). It should be sized so an honest week of Dan's own starting rhythms can reach it.
- **Known residual risk (for the playtest, not a new system):** a trivial rhythm could still make the supply easier to reach. The guard is that rhythms are Dan's own real commitments (D-030); if play shows otherwise, step 3's numbers change, not the rules.
