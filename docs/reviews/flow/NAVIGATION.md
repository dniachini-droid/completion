# Navigation and interaction review

_Branch `claude/keys-visible`, build served on port 4175, reviewed 2026-10-01. Driven with Playwright (Chromium, touch on, 390×844 and 360×780) from a fresh save and from the further-in save (`keysave.json`); every claim below says whether it was clicked or read from the code. Scripts, logs and screenshots: `scratchpad/review/nav/` (`nav1`–`nav6.mjs`, `*.log`, `n*-*.png`). No story text is quoted here; places, records and marks are described only as "a place", "a record", "a mark"._

## How "back" works, in one paragraph

Every screen has an arrow at the top left that names where it goes (Today, Satchel, Map…). The app keeps a trail of the screens you "look through" (Map, Records, Symbols, Week, Daybook, Satchel, Settings, the set-up, the job editor, errands, "I can't start"), and the arrow and the phone's back swipe walk back along it. The day's own moments (a delve, its end, a job's return, a place reached, the morning, the welcome back, the word-cutting, the stair, a niche opened with a Key) are **not** on the trail: entering one wipes the trail, and their arrow goes to Today. Two things follow from that, and most of the problems below are the seams between the two kinds of screen: (1) going from a "moment" to a "look-through" screen puts the moment on the trail, so back returns to it, even when the moment can no longer be shown; (2) "Today" is never allowed to skip a waiting moment, so on a waiting moment the phone's back does nothing at all.

## Findings

### Confirmed bugs (ranked by how much they would hurt)

**1. The Map and a niche opened with a Key trap each other.** _Clicked (nav2, both sizes; `n2-opened-390.png`, `n2-map-after-390.png`)._ Dan taps "Use a Key" on the Map, reads the niche, taps "Back to the Map" (or the arrow, which also says "Map"). The Map comes back, but its arrow now says **"Back"** instead of "Today", and tapping it shows the niche again; its "Back to the Map" shows the Map again; and so on forever. The phone's back swipe does the same. The only way out is to notice the Map's arrow has changed meaning and find another route, or the second-to-last phone back which happens to land on Today. Cause: `App.svelte` — `opened` is not in `LOOK`, so entering it wipes the trail (Today is forgotten), and then `go('map')` from it pushes `{opened}` on the trail, so the Map's back points at the niche. Fix: treat the niche as a look-through screen (add `'opened'` to `LOOK`, and have both its arrow and "Back to the Map" call `go('back')`), or at least never push `opened` onto the trail when leaving it.

**2. Leaving a delve's end for a record or the Satchel loses the end.** _Clicked (nav3, both sizes; `n3-end2-390.png`, `n3-after-back-390.png`)._ At the end of a delve the screen offers story links ("Read…", a small choice) and "One thought parked in the Satchel". Dan taps one, reads, and taps the arrow, which says "Back". He lands on **Today**, not on the end he was reading: the words, the count and any "See where you are" button are gone and never come back. Cause: `App.svelte` `go()` marks the end as seen whenever the screen leaves `delve` for anything but `delve`; on return the Delve screen finds no run and no end and bounces to Today. Fix: mark the end seen only when leaving towards Today/Arrival (`!LOOK.has(to)`), so a look-through screen can return to it. The same shape affects the Morning and Welcome screens ("Read it" → a record → back says "Back" but lands on Today), which matters less since both are meant to be seen once, but the arrow still mislabels where it goes.

**3. "Back to Back".** _Clicked (nav3; `n3-editor-deleted-390.png`)._ Open a job's set-up, tap "Change the job", tap Delete. The editor says the job is gone and offers "Undo" and **"Back to Back"**; its arrow also reads "Back". Cause: `App.svelte` `NAMES` has no entry for `set` (nor `week`'s sheet case is fine), so the label falls to the generic "Back" and `job.back` ("Back to {to}") interpolates it. Fix: give `set` a name (the job's name, or "the set-up"), or special-case the link's text when the label is generic.

**4. On the word-cutting screen, "Later" and the arrow do nothing.** _Read from the code (not clicked: the cut is several game days in; `Cut.svelte:155,215`, `Arrival.svelte:43–47`, `App.svelte:88–90`)._ Before the word is cut, both "Later" and the top-left "Today" call `go('today')`. `go` refuses to skip a waiting arrival, and the cut **is** that waiting arrival, so the screen is set to itself and nothing changes. The code comments describe a `'stay'` argument as "the one way past it: the word left to cut later", but nothing ever passes it. Dan would tap Later, see nothing happen, and have no way out but to cut the word or kill the app (which brings the cut straight back). Fix: `leave('later')` → `go('today', 'stay')`.

### Clumsy flow

**1. Records ⇄ Symbols stop behaving like tabs once a mark is selected.** _Clicked (nav6)._ Records → a record → tap a mark (Symbols opens on it) → tab "Records" → tab "Symbols": the back trail is now Symbols ← Records ← Symbols(mark) ← the record ← Records ← Today. Six presses of back to reach Today, and the first press goes from "Symbols" to "Symbols" (the same title, a different selection), which reads as back doing nothing. Cause: the tab exemption in `go()` requires both screens to have no argument; a mark id defeats it. Fix: treat any records↔marks move as a tab swap (replace rather than push), keeping only list→record as a push.

**2. The Daybook's new page: back is swallowed and the look-ahead offer evaporates.** _Clicked (nav2, nav2b)._ On the first morning of a week the app opens on the new Daybook page with "Look ahead · Plan it for me · Not now". The phone's back swipe does nothing (the page is a waiting moment). The arrow does leave, but marks the page read, and after that the offer is gone from the page for good even though Dan never answered it (`offer = fresh && …`; `leave()` calls `read()`). "Offered once" was the intent (D-045), but leaving by the arrow isn't an answer. Fix: let the offer survive until Dan picks one of its three answers, or make the arrow count as "Not now" and say so.

**3. Screens that were opened from the Satchel or Week drop you on Today.** _Clicked (nav3)._ Tick a job off in the Satchel: the return screen's arrow and button both say Today, and go there, so Dan loses his place in the Satchel. "I can't start" from the Satchel's menu: the arrow correctly says "Satchel", but "Not now" goes to Today. The return screen is a moment, so Today is defensible, but it should at least be the same destination from the arrow and the button, and "Not now" should mean "back where I was".

**4. A niche opened with a Key can be read once only.** _Code (`Map.svelte:61` filters `!v.story.opened.has(x.id)`; `Opened.svelte`)._ Once Dan has left the Opened screen (including by a reload, a phone call, or bug 1's confusion), the niche is no longer listed on the Map and its words are not reachable anywhere unless it carried a record. Places reached on foot can be read again from the Map and from Today's place name (D-135); opened niches should get the same "read again" treatment.

**5. The phone's back swipe does nothing on any waiting moment.** _Clicked (nav5: fresh arrival, the morning; nav2: the Daybook's new page; code for Welcome)._ On a freshly reached place, the morning, the welcome back and a new Daybook page, a swipe from the left edge is silently ignored (trail empty → `go('today')` → redirected back to the same screen). The arrow works. On the iPhone, where that swipe is muscle memory, the app looks frozen for a beat. Fix: let the phone's back on a waiting moment do what its arrow does (mark seen and go on), or at least on the arrival/morning/welcome where there is nothing to lose.

**6. The arrow's label is generic ("Back") exactly where it matters most.** _Clicked._ "Back" appears on: the editor from the set-up (bug 3), the Satchel after "Keep going" or a parked-thought link, the Map after a niche (bug 1), a record from a place or a delve's end, Symbols from a record. Every other arrow names its destination; these are the ones where Dan is deepest and least sure.

**7. Two controls for one exit, on most of the moments.** _Seen._ Arrival: arrow "Today" + "Back to today". Opened: arrow "Map" + "Back to the Map". Step: arrow + "Back to today". Morning: arrow + "On to today". Stair: arrow "Today" + a "Today" button. Delve's end: arrow + "Back to today". Records: arrow + "Done reading" (these two differ: Done reading goes to Today, the arrow goes back). The UI review listed this; it still holds. Harmless on a wide phone; on a small one the arrow is the only exit that doesn't scroll away.

**8. "Keep going" / "Go down" / "Recurring jobs" all open the Satchel under other names.** From a place, "Keep going" goes to the Satchel; from the stair, "Go down" goes to the Satchel; from the Week, "Recurring jobs" opens the Satchel scrolled to its last section (the title still says The Satchel). None of them say so, and the arrow afterwards says "Back" (first two) or "This week". Fine as a design once learned, but Dan asked for seamless: naming where the button goes ("Keep going · the Satchel") would help.

### Minor polish

- **Job menu with its calendar at 360×780** (`n1-menu-cal-360.png`): the menu is taller than the screen; "Delete" sits at the very bottom and "Cancel" is below the fold inside a menu that scrolls by itself. On 390×844 it just fits. Consider opening the calendar in place of the menu's other items, or capping the menu to leave Cancel visible.
- **A slid row at 360 hides the job's name** (`n1-swipe-360.png`): "Not today" and "Delete" at 96 px each push the name off the left edge, so Dan can't see which job he is about to delete. The UI review noted this; it still holds.
- **Reload / restart mid-screen**: the screen is never remembered (always Today, or what waits). A running or paused delve is kept in the save and comes back ✔; a half-typed editor is lost; a Satchel list draft is kept on blur ✔. Acceptable for a game, but a restart on the Opened screen loses the niche's words for good (clumsy 4).
- **Naming**: "Back to today" (delve end, arrival, step, welcome) vs "On to today" (morning) vs the arrow "Today"; "Back to the Map" (Opened) vs "Back to MAP" (read-again, built from the arrow label, so it comes out in capitals); "Change the job" (set-up, Week sheet) vs "Edit" (menu, Satchel swipe) for the same editor; "Symbols" as a tab but "What you can read" as its title and "Records" vs "What you've found". None wrong, but a settled vocabulary would read calmer.
- **Daybook pager**: "Earlier" pushes a step on the trail, "Later" replaces; so back after Earlier returns to the newer page, but back after Later returns to wherever the Daybook was opened from. Pick one.
- **Settings' only link** to the trial controls uses the same long sentence as that screen's title, so the arrow says "Settings" and the heading repeats the link: fine, just wordy.
- **Today's foot link "Daybook" on a fresh save** opens an empty page with one line. The UI review suggested hiding it until a page exists; still open.
- **Welcome back has no arrow** while every other screen has one; its only ways on are the big button and the Map link.

### Things I tried that are fine

- **Quick double taps** (finger, 120–150 ms apart) on a Today row, Begin, Finish here and Not yet: one decision each time ✔ (nav4). The `taps.ts` guard works.
- **Press and hold** on Today, Satchel and Week rows opens the job menu; the finger's lift and a tap within half a second are not taken as a choice; a tap outside after that closes it ✔. Hold on the Week's row doesn't open the sheet ✔.
- **Slide** a row with a finger: the two actions appear; tapping the row or any other row closes it; the "Delete twice" problem from the break-it review no longer reproduced (the row list didn't lose a second job) ✔.
- **Map**: drag only engages once the region outgrows the screen (the field reports 390 wide, 548 tall vs 485 visible, so it scrolls vertically only) ✔. Picking a light never moves the sky ✔.
- **Delve across screens**: Today's arrow during a run shows a single "Back to the delve" card; Map and other screens during a run return correctly; a delve ending while elsewhere is shown ✔.
- **"Add a job"** opens the Satchel with its box focused ✔.
- **Errand run** chain: Today/Satchel → pick list → set-up (arrow "Errand run") → back → back ✔.
- **Week** sheet, Next week / This week, Map from the Week: all return where expected ✔.
- **VoiceOver labels**: every icon-only control I found has a label (the gear, the Week's "+", the calendar's ‹ ›, the count and stepper buttons, the tick circles, the Map's lights, a record's marks, the painting layer, Today's place name); new screens announce their title through a live region ✔.
- **Story links from a fresh arrival** → a record → arrow → the arrival (still fresh) ✔; "Done reading" → the arrival ✔ (back-from-record still passes).
