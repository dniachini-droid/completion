## Notes

- Week 22 and week 23 are one week apart on purpose: the sign, then the face.
- Week 25.B is the only time in the year Dan and the Custodian are in the same passage with nothing between them. No line is spoken; the app describes the wall taking his shoulder and lets him be passed. He is *behind* Dan for the first time, which is the whole reveal of R8 felt in the body two months before it is read.
- ECHO's blank is reachable from week 14 with both signs held; if Dan cuts it then, 17.A plays in week 14 and week 17 gains a step instead.
- Dark rooms in this region: the Hold's cloth (wk 16) and the child's half-cut lesson (wk 18); each is paired in-week with a kind line (S10; the wall of lamps).
