## Notes

- After week 52 the app's line at camp is the lamp, and nothing more is asked. New regions are later content (`REVELATION_MAP.md`, "After year 1").
- Week 41–43's growing record is what makes week 51's "I began your record" a re-reading rather than a new fact.
- The ending is a re-reading of the whole year in three places: the finest cut (every mark old), the list (every ring and every name), the lesson-wall (the first line).
