# Regions 4 and 5, visit by visit — SPOILERS

> **Sealed (D-015).** Weeks 27–52: below the three doors, the last door, the Seed, and the ending. Same format, density and voice rules as `ARRIVALS_REGION1.md` and `ARRIVALS_REGION3.md`: a Normal week is about five arrivals and as many steps, each authored, with the camp line, the morning line, the "I can't start" teaser and the week-close glimpse; *(Key)* and *(High)* only in the Kind column; one open cell a week where an experiment rhymes with the room. The visit rate is the same as above the doors. The deep is sparser in *fragments*, not in visits: the extra visits are objects, re-reads surfaced by the app, and things to try. Fragment ids, signs and Key-sealed things follow `REVELATION_MAP.md` weeks 27–52, `NICHES.md` and `SCRIPT.md` §6. Quoted record text is the *rendering* at the signs held that week; a bracketed gap is a glyph Dan does not yet have (LOUD until week 32; NAME until week 30, so rings stay *[ring]*; ONE-WHO until week 36; GIVE renders *give/given* until it resolves at week 35 and *put out* after, record by record as `LIVES.md` §13 authors it).
> App names for places in these regions (`SITE.md`, regions 4–5): the first hall below the doors is **the reading-tables**; the passage past its lintel is **the soundless passage**; the rooms beyond that passage's door are **the quiet rooms** (from week 34); the lift's stops are **the second level** and **the last level**; the room of racks is **the tool-room**; the Surveyor's chamber is **the shut door** and, inside, **the chamber**; the memorial walls stay **the walls of rings**; then **the last stair**, **the last landing**, **her last camp**, **the last door**, **the Seed-place**, **the way up**. Sealed names (the Builders' deep works, the quiet chambers, the memoir, the Copyist, the binders) stay in the *Carries* column and the notes.
> The four hooks keep their fixed names from first sight: his is *a hook closed on a dot*; the makers' is *a hook closed on nothing* (17.B); hers is *a hook with a tail*; the giving-mark's element is *a hook, open*. Readers are "the first", "the second", "the fourth", or their object; the app never says whose hand, never states an age, never names a motive, never says "Key", never talks calendar, and never uses an English Cut word as a title.
> Open cells (`SCRIPT.md` §5.1, §8.3): one is shown per week where an experiment rhymes with the room (27.4 *read here*, 29.4 *wake sleep*, 32.4 *douse*, 34.3 *keep fire*, 35.4 *give light*). The app never lists what they take; the *Carries* column records the authored answer.
