## The jobs, in order
1. **The fixes from the outside review** (D-035; `sealed/REVIEW_EXTERNAL_1.md` and Claude's verdicts there). **Top priority:** the late game must never appear to promise a meaningful choice the fixed story can't honour. The fixed ending and canon stay; fix presentation and interaction, not branching. Then the precision fixes (the late story's presentation, one character's reasoning, how one mechanism is explained in the fiction, one location detail, the wording of two internal rules). Follow §55 for anything that touches canon. Record each fix and why.
2. **Week 6 of the clue ledger** (D-023): complete, with every planted clue's predetermined answer (rule 6) and fair-play checks (`sealed/FAIR_PLAY.md`).
3. **Before → now:** decide whether story weeks 1–6 change any earlier reading. Record a one-word answer (yes / no) for the Dan-facing tracker, and the reason in sealed files only.
4. **The MVP's content, written in full** for six story weeks plus the run-ahead (`product/MVP.md` → "The words and pictures it needs"; `BALANCING.md` §2–5, §7 and "Content this asks the story to supply"): named places (30–35, name and line), camps with a view (15, each with its one thing to look at), record fragments (15, two lives), sealed things (30), signs with 3–4 candidate meanings each (8–12), the word(s) with the cutting cinematic (1–2; the first in week 2–3), finds (~70), passage lines for the open route (~120), the week close's "learned" lines (18) and the month's "so far" (2), teasers for "I can't start" (~30, from just ahead, never new story). Keep the story clock: order, at most one story week per calendar week, places ahead by one week at most, signs never early.
5. **A painting brief for every named place and camp view** (D-054, D-057): for the painting kit (`technical/TECH_DECISIONS.md` → paintings). Per place: forms, scale, material, the light (one source), camera and vanishing point, the one thing to look at, what moves (fog, flame, glints, water, dust), and one **signature** that makes it unlike the places around it. Direction D's rules (`design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `ART_DIRECTION.md`): full-bleed, painted not drawn, cold stone and violet light, gold as the day turns, no red, no figure unless the story needs one.
6. **Shape it for the build.** Every item gets a **stable id** and **when it may appear** (story week, order, prerequisites), using the types in `technical/DATA_MODEL.md`, so Phase 9 can turn it into app content without re-deciding anything. Plain language in the app's own voice; wording is not final until the language pass (D-046), but it must not sound stilted or generated (D-031).
7. **If needed,** the one line of the open game bible about how the doors fill (D-035), reworded without changing anything Dan already knows.

## How to work
- Research-led and careful, as in the Phase 3 story run (D-016–D-019). Use subagents for independent review passes: (a) continuity against `WORLD_TRUTH.md`, `TIMELINE.md` and `REVELATION_MAP.md`; (b) fair play and rule 6; (c) pacing against `BALANCING.md` and `PACING.md`; (d) the late-choice promise (job 1's top priority); (e) voice. Fix what they find; one more review round if the first found anything serious.
- Commit and push after each job, with **spoiler-free** messages (`narrative: story job, week 6 ledger`, `narrative: MVP place lines`), never quoting content.
- Decisions: add **one spoiler-free entry** to `docs/DECISIONS.md` at the end (fetch `origin/main` first and take the next free D-number; details go in the sealed files). Update `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` spoiler-free: the story job done, the before → now answer, the content budget met, painting briefs ready.
- Finish with a **pull request into `main`**, its text spoiler-free (what jobs were done and counts only). **Don't merge it**: Dan approves the merge from the main session (D-006).
- Your last message is spoiler-free too: Dan may read it.
