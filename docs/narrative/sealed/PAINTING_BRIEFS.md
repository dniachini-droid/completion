## The rules every brief obeys (direction D; `design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `ART_DIRECTION.md`)

- **Full-bleed**, portrait, painted edge to edge at iPhone Pro Max resolution; never a scene inside a frame.
- **Painted, not drawn:** lit masses, relief, haze and depth; no outlined objects; strokes belong to the interface only.
- **One light source per scene** (named in each brief), **one vanishing point**. The Site's own light is **cold violet place-light** (`--violet`, `--cold`, fog); the only warm thing is the **clay lamp** (`--flame`) and, once the word is cut, the **cups**, whose flames are the lamp's colour (hall.js `hlCup`). In a lit hall the line of cups counts as one source.
- **Gold as the day turns:** every arrival's painting takes the kit's gold floor-wash as its day completes (a live layer, not baked); camp views end at the gold of camp.
- **No red anywhere.** Salt is grey, white and pink at the edges only where the brief says; rust is brown.
- **No figure** in any of these scenes: the story needs none before week 8. No hand, shadow or silhouette of one.
- **Cold stone**, not warm: the stone reads blue-grey to violet in shadow; objects of the readers' ages (bronze, wool, paper, tin) are the only other colours, muted.
- **Leave the text zone readable** where the arrival's line sits (the lower third), per the kit's automatic check.
- **Nothing the player hasn't reached is painted in detail**: what lies ahead is haze, a shape or a glow.
- **The one thing to look at** is where the eye lands first: the brightest value or the sharpest edge in the frame.
- **The signature** is the one thing no neighbouring place has, so a week of places never becomes the same room with a different number.

**States.** Some places are painted in more than one state; the kit bakes each and the app picks by the facts: the hall **dark / lit** (before and after `b-3.A`), the lintel **sealed / open**, the great door **shut / open** (`b-7.C`), a sealed thing **shut / opened** (the count's strokes filled, the lid off) where the place shows it.

## 3. Notes for the kit and the critique

- **Reuse without sameness.** Many places share the hall, the salt or the camp; each brief's camera, look-at and signature is chosen so no two neighbouring scenes read the same. The critique (`design/tools/CRITIQUE_PROMPT.md`) should check each week's five side by side for that.
- **The hall has two lives.** Everything in `st-hall` before `b-3.A` is lit by the clay lamp alone; after it, by the cups. Bake both states for every hall scene a player can see in both (`pt-b-1.A`, `pt-pl-w1-below-the-lamp`, `pt-pl-w5-ledge-lip` is after only; `pt-cv-03` before only; `pt-cv-04` after only).
- **The lamp never flickers.** Its flame layer moves (a slow rise and settle) but does not gutter in draughts; the cups the same. The one exception is `pt-b-7.C`'s single flicker as the cold air comes up, which is the draught at the cups, not the lamp.
- **Paper and objects of her age** are the only saturated whites and the only printed or marker letters (HILL, DAY 1, 40 FT in chalk). Keep them small; they are texture, not signage.
- **Later (month 11), for fair play:** the Seed's painting must make the lamp's light the live layer that reveals the fine cut on a surface the kit otherwise paints as smooth glass (`STORY_JOB.md` §1).
