## Voice rules for the app's lines

_The language pass (2026-09-24, D-074; Dan: "written like a story", "full sentences that are a joy to read", "think J. R. R. Tolkien") changed how the lines are said, never what they plant: every rule below about facts still holds._

- **Whole sentences, like a told tale.** Every line has its verbs: no telegraphic fragments, no clipped lists of nouns, no chains of colons and semicolons (a colon only before quoted words, her pencil, or a short list). Measured, warm and concrete, with rhythm; plain words, never fake-archaic or fantasy-speak. Present tense for what is in front of Dan; *you* may stand, look, lift, follow.
- Say what is there. Name the drawable things plainly. No adjectives of mood ("eerie", "ancient"), and no new simile or detail that would say who, when or how big: a rewrite changes the saying, never the facts.
- Never "you should", never "while you were gone", never a count of what was missed. Never an inventory of what Dan holds or lacks: that is the marks screen's job.
- A step ends by showing something ahead that Dan can see but not yet reach.
- An arrival ends on the thing Dan will remember tomorrow.
- Words that are the Cut's are shown as marks, never spoken by the app in English. Titles too.
- Fixed names for the three hooks, from first sight: his hand-mark is *a hook closed on a dot*; the element of the giving-mark is *a hook, open* (the full sign: *the hook and the drop*); hers is *a hook with a tail*.
