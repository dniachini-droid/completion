# Region 1, visit by visit — SPOILERS

> **Sealed (D-015).** The content the first playable actually shows: for weeks 1–5 (week 5 as margin) (a Normal week: about five arrivals, two or three with a fragment, three to five Keys), every **step** line (after a main job; 20–40 seconds), every **arrival** scene (day complete; under a minute), the **small choice** where there is one, the **camp** line (bedtime by the chosen time), the **"I can't start" teaser**, and the **week close** glimpse. Written in the app's voice: plain, warm, concrete, never translating, never suggesting an age, never addressing Dan's life (P14; LIVES §0). Fragment ids and sign arrivals follow `REVELATION_MAP.md` and `SCRIPT.md` §6; the truth behind each clue is in `CLUE_LEDGER.md`.
> Low weeks stretch this over more days; High weeks pull the next week's *places* forward (the deep-push rows) and never its signs.
> Quoted record text is the rendering at the signs held that week; a bracketed gap is a glyph Dan does not yet have.
> A row whose **Kind** carries *(Key)* plays when a Key lands, in the week's order (`NICHES.md`); the line itself never says "Key". Where a teaser has two lines, the first shows until the named row has played.

_Written 2026-09-23; revised the same day after the first-hour review (`REVIEW.md`)._

## Voice rules for the app's lines

- Say what is there. One drawable noun per line. No adjectives of mood ("eerie", "ancient").
- Never "you should", never "while you were gone", never a count of what was missed. Never an inventory of what Dan holds or lacks: that is the marks screen's job.
- A step ends by showing something ahead that Dan can see but not yet reach.
- An arrival ends on the thing Dan will remember tomorrow.
- Words that are the Cut's are shown as marks, never spoken by the app in English. Titles too.
- Fixed names for the three hooks, from first sight: his hand-mark is *a hook closed on a dot*; the element of the giving-mark is *a hook, open* (the full sign: *the hook and the drop*); hers is *a hook with a tail*.

## Notes for Phase 8

- Every arrival above is a *place*; every step is *movement plus one thing seen*. Nothing is ever a task.
- The choice column is always one tap and never gates progress.
- The "carries" column is the only place ids appear; the app never shows them.
- Sign arrival is by Key, inside the named sealed thing; if Keys come faster, the extra Keys open the next listed sealed thing, never the next sign early.
- The first word (3.A) is four taps with one line each, in the order given; the place's answer is the confirmation, and no title or line names the word.
