# Region 1, visit by visit — SPOILERS

> **Sealed (D-015).** The content the first playable actually shows: for weeks 1–4 (a Normal week: about five arrivals, two or three with a fragment, three to five Keys), every **step** line (after a main job; 20–40 seconds), every **arrival** scene (day complete; under a minute), the **small choice** where there is one, the **camp** line (bedtime by the chosen time), the **"I can't start" teaser**, and the **week close** glimpse. Written in the app's voice: plain, warm, concrete, never translating, never suggesting an age, never addressing Dan's life (P14; LIVES §0). Fragment ids and sign arrivals follow `REVELATION_MAP.md` and `SCRIPT.md` §6; the truth behind each clue is in `CLUE_LEDGER.md`.
> Low weeks stretch this over more days; High weeks pull the next week's *places* forward (the deep-push rows) and never its signs.

_Written 2026-09-23._

## Voice rules for the app's lines

- Say what is there. One drawable noun per line. No adjectives of mood ("eerie", "ancient").
- Never "you should", never "while you were gone", never a count of what was missed.
- A step ends by showing something ahead that Dan can see but not yet reach.
- An arrival ends on the thing Dan will remember tomorrow.
- Words that are the Cut's are shown as marks, never spoken by the app in English.
