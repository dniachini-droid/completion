## Notes

- Every arrival above is a *place*, with at most one record staged in it; every step is *movement plus one thing seen*. The three turns (8.B, 9.B, 11.B) are places, not marks-screen events: the pivot surfaces one record at the visit (SCRIPT §10) and the rest re-render at the morning or when reached.
- The words (7.A, 7.C, 10.A, 13.B) are four taps with one line each, in the order given; the place's answer is the confirmation, and no title or line names the word in English.
- L9 is read after 8.B (the double notation seen before her English explains it), as L5 is read after S3 and L10 before the NOT Key.
- The Surveyor's door: the inference (shut from this side, by the tally's hand, while the man inside lived) is never stated. The record carries it (V6 with its hand-mark), and the one physical clue is handed over once, at 12.C: stone chips on Dan's side of the door (C-51a). The wk-12 close and the wk-13 teaser describe the door; the wk-13 camp describes the lamp.
- The figure at week 8 is one sentence at the end of 8.A, standing, and does not vanish (REVELATION_MAP wk 8; SITE region 2; CHARACTERS §1).
