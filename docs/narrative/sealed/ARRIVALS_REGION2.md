## Notes

- Dark rooms (the book's ledge in week 11; the shut door in week 13) are each paired in-week with a kind line (L12; S8, the child's tally of days).
- The two verbs on the shut door (SHUT ×2) stay glyphs until month 6, when they turn in the same week as KEEP: the mid-point's second dark re-read.
- Week 13's MOVE-STONE is the third word; if Keys ran fast, its blank was reachable from week 10 but the signs MOVE and STONE gate it (STONE week 7, MOVE week 10): the earliest honest week is 10, the authored week 13.
