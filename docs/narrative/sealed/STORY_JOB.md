No screen lists the blanks as options; no line says *choose, decide, ending, forever*; no confirmation. (WORLD_TRUTH §10.3; ARR45 50.A notes; SCRIPT §8.3; SITE region 5.)
  2. **The answering word is a real cut with an authored, informative consequence**, a visit of its own (ARR45 **50.B**): the lock; the ring stopping at Dan's feet, as it did on the last door's sill (44.A: nothing carries past a quiet sill); his voice once (*It has not carried.   3. **The agency is epistemic.** What the late game asks is *is he right?*, and every piece of evidence is already on the walls (Z2, X-answerer, L17, L18, L22's doubt). The cut follows understanding; the app never presents it as a verdict and never says he was wrong (see "ending tone" below).

## 4. The MVP's content, written in full (`MVP_CONTENT.md`)

**Creative calls, and why:**
1. **The shape of a story week for the build.** A week is a *route* of five named places (§1): the story arrivals already written, pinned where they were, and new places between them. BALANCING asks for about five places a week, and the visit tables had two or three arrivals a week, because they were written before the numbers. The new places carry no fragment (so "about half carry a fragment" holds) and each ends on a sealed thing now in view, which gives every day's arrival the heart's last link: *a sealed thing ahead you can see*.
2. **What a new place may be.** Only a real spot in the approved geography (SITE, LOCATIONS), usually where a NICHES item already sits (the pick niche, below the lamp, the hollow, the recess above the cot, the wall-shelf, her folder), or a view the files already imply (the salt lit; the far end; the worn steps; the second landing; the square gallery). No new rooms, no new lore.
3. **Five sealed things added** so each week has five Key rows (NICHES rows 6), each an object already implied by canon: the first's oil flask (S1: "as far as the oil"), her head torch (L5's dying batteries; this also answers quietly how she saw before the lamps woke), her foil blanket (*in case I'm an idiot*: her voice, and her caution before she stopped going up), her dictionary (her trade), the crew's water jar (V1: the men stopped at the light). NICHES 2.4 became a named place (a smooth place opened by a Key read oddly).
4. **Counts on her things are slates of the hall's stone** laid over them. The canon rule says a count works only in laced stone, and ARR1 already had counts on her tin box: this is the smallest reading that keeps both, and it says something true about him (he sealed her camp for the next reader, as he keeps every reader's things). Clarification, not retcon; the app never says who cut them.
5. **The square gallery holds counts** because the hill's rock is laced; it has no lamp-cups because the makers did not cut it, so its light comes from the round side. Both follow from WORLD_TRUTH §5.1; written down so the paintings and passage lines agree.
6. **Finds are the world's small change**: 72 things already true, sorted by stretch, each with its truth in a clause. Most are hers (paper, tape, tea: she is the reader closest to Dan, and her funny, practical mess makes the camp a place someone lived), the first's (bronze, wool, salt, hide), the road crew's (hobnails, a jar, a whetstone) and the railway shaft's (chalk in feet).
