# Factions and minds — SPOILERS

> **Sealed (D-015).** There are no faction meters (D-012). Factions here are *positions in an argument*, and the argument is the story's spine: keep or send. Human institutions appear only as pressure on a life and never enter the Site.

_Written 2026-09-23. Must agree with `WORLD_TRUTH.md`._

## The argument

| Position | Held by | Thesis | What it built or did | Where Dan meets it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The binders** ("we who keep") | The Builders' majority; the chief binder (the Custodian, once) | The language must be kept quiet and readable in one bound place: a school and a warning. What is kept survives. | The Quiet: counting doors, quiet chambers, the lintels (KEEP VOICE-NOT), the Hold, the prepared word KEEP-ALL | The whole Site; the Warning Hall (month 5–6); the Builders' memoir |
| **The answerers** ("we who send") | The Builders' minority; the Custodian, now | The Speakers were sent and reached us: sending is how a people survives. What is kept is buried; what is sent is heard. Being found is being joined. | Nothing built; one wall of argument in the Council Gallery; the prepared word VOICE-GIVE-FAR | The Council Gallery (month 5, half readable; month 6 in full); the Custodian's thesis in the Linguist's notes (months 8–9); the Seed (month 12) |
| **The readers** | The five before Dan | Not a thesis; an accident repeated. Each wanted something different (home; a road; a clean copy; a tunnel; an answer) and each learned a piece. | The lamp, the report, the book, the log, the lesson-wall | Everywhere, in order |
| **The Speakers** | A gift with no one behind it | "We make all of it here: what is put out; what is taken in; what is kept." And, folded at the end: what speaks loud is found. | The seed; the language itself | The seed's text (months 7–12) |
| **The Listeners** | A process, not a party | None. | Three endings and a handful of anomalies | Never |

The player's ending is the binders' word cut by a reader. It is not a third position; it is the first position, finally finished, by the only kind of person the binders designed it for.

## What the app must never do with any of this

No reputation, allegiance or alignment for Dan. He is never asked to *side* with binders or answerers as a mechanic. The argument is felt through records and answered once, at the Seed, by the word he cuts, and the story fixes that word (WORLD_TRUTH §10).
