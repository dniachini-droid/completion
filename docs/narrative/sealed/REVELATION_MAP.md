## Arc level: year 1 and beyond

### Arc 1 — The Lamp (weeks 1–4). *The place answers.*
Region 1. Two lives (Salt-Cutter, Linguist), one lamp, one figure. First word week 2–3; the Lamp Door; the head of the Counting Stair seen. The turn: R1, R2. Dan can name the promise by the end: many came down; one figure met them all; she wrote to me.

### Arc 2 — The Stair (months 2–3). *Everyone met him, and he let them.*
Region 2. The Surveyor's galleries (V), the Loud Room (E, the Copyist's book, C), the Water, the Reading Room. OPEN-WAY, MOVE-STONE. The turn: R3, R4. The category shifts from *a place with a ghost* to *a lineage of readers and one teacher*. First loud-use/anomaly pairing noticed. The Surveyor's fate half-read.

### Arc 3 — The Hold (months 4–6). *A school with a warning.*
Region 3: the Builders' halls. The memoir begins (B). ECHO (the blast in the Loud Room; the Engineer's fall). The Hold (the laid-out dead; the Engineer's watch). The Council Gallery (the two positions). The Warning Hall (half readable). **KEEP at month 6: the mid-point.** The Landing: the face. The turn: R5, R6. The category shifts from *tomb* to *hold*.

### Arc 4 — The Deep (months 7–9). *The help was the antagonist.*
The three counting doors (Keys, months 6–7), then region 4. SEED, LOUD, NAME. The door-maker's plan. Below the doors the rings stop; B7 by the last lift. The memoir's "I". The Surveyor's shut chamber (his own hand; region 2, opened now with HOLD). The workshop (region 3): REMAIN on the wall, his hand-print. The Linguist's transcription of his thesis. QUIET. The turn: R7, R8. Dread inverts: every gate he wanted open. The problem is real; the cure is his.

### Arc 5 — The Seed (months 10–12). *Keep or answer.*
The Linguist's last camp against the last door (R9). The two words. The last counting door (a great real milestone's Key; QUIET, then OPEN-WAY LOUD; the lintel: *be loud here and not be heard*). The Seed-place, alone, with the lamp: the finest cut (R9.5, month 11). ECHO once: the quietening. His speech, down the lacing from the doors. KEEP-ALL. The way up: his outline in the wall; the list on the door (R10). The coda at camp.

### After year 1 (not designed now; D-004)
The story is complete. Further content would be new regions of the Site to read (the Builders' great age; other readers' ages the Custodian recorded but Dan did not reach; the seed's text in full), never a reopened ending. Trap doors (RESEARCH lesson 7): any of V, C, E can be shortened if the year runs short; B and L cannot.

## Rules the map obeys

- No reveal before its sign; no sign before its Key; no Key from anything but Dan's real week (D-013).
- Every reveal points back to a specific earlier fragment, named in the table.
- The darkest rooms (the Loud Room, week 11; the shut door, week 13; the Surveyor's chamber, month 8–9; the last camp, month 10) are each paired within their week with a kind fragment (C1's dry devotion; L12; S8's old age; the lesson-wall).
- "I can't start" teasers are always *from just ahead* and never name a consequence.
- Nothing in the Site changes in Dan's absence. Fresh cuts (K1–K3) are already there when he arrives; the app never says "since you left".
