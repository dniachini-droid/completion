## Arc level: year 1 and beyond

### Arc 1 — The Lamp (weeks 1–4). *The place answers.*
Region 1. Two lives (Salt-Cutter, Linguist), one lamp, one figure. First word week 2–3; the Lamp Door; the head of the Counting Stair seen. The turn: R1, R2. Dan can name the promise by the end: many came down; one figure met them all; she wrote to me.

### Arc 2 — The Stair (months 2–3). *Everyone met him, and he let them.*
Region 2. The Surveyor's galleries (V), the Loud Room (E, the Copyist's book, C), the Water, the Reading Room. OPEN-WAY, MOVE-STONE. The turn: R3, R4. The category shifts from *a place with a ghost* to *a lineage of readers and one teacher*. First loud-use/anomaly pairing noticed. The Surveyor's fate half-read.

### Arc 3 — The Hold (months 4–6). *A school with a warning.*
Region 3: the Builders' halls. The memoir begins (B). ECHO (the blast in the Loud Room; the laying-out in the Hold). The Hold (the laid-out dead; the Engineer's boots and log). The Council Gallery (the two positions). The Warning Hall (half readable). **KEEP at month 6: the mid-point.** The Landing: the face. The turn: R5, R6. The category shifts from *tomb* to *hold*.

### Arc 4 — The Deep (months 7–9). *The help was the antagonist.*
The three counting doors (Keys, months 6–7), then region 4. SEED, LOUD, NAME. The door-maker's plan. Below the doors the rings stop; B7 by the last lift. The memoir's "I". The Surveyor's shut chamber (his own hand; region 2, opened now with HOLD). The workshop (region 3): REMAIN on the wall, his hand-print. The Linguist's transcription of his thesis. QUIET. The turn: R7, R8. Dread inverts: every gate he wanted open. The problem is real; the cure is his.

### Arc 5 — The Seed (months 10–12). *Keep or answer.*
The Linguist's last camp against the last door (R9). The two words. The last counting door (its count is the world on the hand: returns, stroke by stroke, the largest count in the Site; QUIET, then OPEN-WAY LOUD; the lintel: *be loud here and not be heard*). The Seed-place, alone, with the lamp set on the count: the finest cut (R9.5, month 11). ECHO once, its own visit: the quietening. His speech, down the lacing from the doors (week 49). KEEP-ALL (week 50). The way up, its own visit (week 51): the deep halls still lit; his outline in the wall; the list on the door (R10). The coda at camp (week 52); then, on a later pass of the first door, K6 with no new mark.

### After year 1 (not designed now; D-004)
The story is complete. Further content would be new regions of the Site to read (the Builders' great age; other readers' ages the Custodian recorded but Dan did not reach; the seed's text in full), never a reopened ending. Trap doors (RESEARCH lesson 7): any of V, C, E can be shortened if the year runs short; S, L and B cannot.

## Rules the map obeys

- No reveal before its sign; no sign before its Key; no Key from anything but Dan's real week (D-013).
- Every reveal points back to a specific earlier fragment, named in the table.
- The darkest rooms (the Loud Room, week 11; the shut door, week 13; the Surveyor's chamber, month 8–9; the last camp, month 10) are each paired within their week with a kind fragment (C1's dry devotion; L12; S8's old age; the lesson-wall).
- "I can't start" teasers are always *from just ahead* and never name a consequence.
- No state in the Site deteriorates in Dan's absence, and nothing marks an absence as lateness. His cuts (K1–K3) are already there when he arrives; the app never says "since you left". The one designed change is the first door's face (weeks 41–50), which is keeping, not waiting: K6, his record of Dan on the first door, grows by one mark each time Dan comes back down, and beside it, from week 43, K4's rings accumulate (the halls' rings, in the halls' order; a column longer each week), cut while Dan is below; the app describes the new marks and still never says "since you left". K4's head, the five readers' rings and the empty ring are cut while Dan is at the Seed (week 50) and read on the way up (week 51). After the binding K6 does not grow: the first later visit past the first door shows no new mark, described once, no gloss.
