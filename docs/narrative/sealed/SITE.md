## Shape

The Site is a descent. It was cut by a people who lived in the dark and thought in touch and sound, and who stood twice a man's height, so its halls are long, high and rounded at the edges like the inside of a shell, its side passages low, and every surface within reach of a long hand is cut with script; readers call the cutting "true" (never "square"). Water is rare and still. Air is dry and moves slowly from below. Nothing rots. The lamps are cut into the walls (a fork-in-a-cup every few paces) and wake in sequence when LIGHT is cut nearby, so a lit hall reads as a line of small flames going away round a corner. **Lamps rule:** wall-lamps woken by a bearer stay lit while that bearer is inside the Site and sleep when they leave for long; the deep halls are lit because *he* woke them and has never left; the upper hall is dark for Dan until he cuts LIGHT; the clay lamp alone is always lit, because "Lit." is cut on it in his hand. Sound carries *inward and down* along the lacing, never out (WORLD_TRUTH rule 6a).

Human intrusions come in from four sides at three depths: the salt crack (high, north), the road tunnel (high, south), the railway blast (low, east), and the trial shaft the company sank from the hilltop after the engineer's reports and capped at the closure (the Linguist's way). Dan enters by the shaft.

## Gate rules (D-013, made concrete)

- **Lintels** (word-doors): a doorway-shape with marks beside a rod-shaped blank and only stone beneath. Need a word; they show which by the signs already cut.
- **Counts** (counting locks): a tally that fills; on niches, sealed records, small chambers, side doors, falls that a lock holds shut. Need a Key. In fiction they count returns from the surface, and they know their maker: he passes any lock in his domain; only the four counting doors do not know him. These are how up to ~250 sealed things a year are supplied: each holds a record, an object or a chorus fragment, authored against `LIVES.md` and `CHARACTERS.md`.
- **Great doors** (both): the Lower Door in the Lamp Hall (a shortcut descent; month 2), and the region boundaries from 3 down.
- **Counting doors** (passage; the safeguard): exactly four. Three at the foot of the Builders' halls (months 6–7) and the last door before the Seed (months 10–11). They know their bearer, open freely to anyone going up, and are shut to a new bearer whatever anyone before did. **The Custodian cannot pass them and has not been below them since his people ended.**
- **Falls and blocks**: need MOVE-STONE or HOLD (a word).
- **Blanks and open cells** (SCRIPT §8.3): a power word can only be cut into a rod-shaped blank that names it; open cells on many walls take any two-sign word and answer with one line.
- A gate that lacks what it needs simply waits. Nothing is ever locked *behind* Dan, and nothing shuts for good on anyone: counts open again to anyone who goes out and comes back.

## Where he can be

Regions 1–3 only: the Lamp Hall and its galleries, the Stair, the Surveyor's galleries, the Water, the Reading Room, the Loud Room, the Builders' halls down to the Landing and the three doors. He is never at camp, never behind Dan, and always further in *until the doors*. Below the doors, Dan finds his hand only in what he cut before he came up (B7) and in the ring-density: the rings thin to almost nothing past the three doors.

## Forks (the small choices, D-010)

At a fork the app names what each way is *like*, never what it holds: "the way with the cord dropped on it" / "the way the lamps are colder". Each way leads to a different life's records first; both are eventually reachable. Forks never gate story order: the authored sign order (SCRIPT §6) is the same whichever way is taken. The first fork is at the Water (month 2): the Reading Room's way or the Loud Room's way.

## Camp, absence, and the rules of the place

- **Camp** is the Lamp Hall, by the lamp, for the whole year (the evening close). Bedtime by the chosen time → something at camp in the morning: a line of an old record newly readable, a mark on the map, or a chorus fragment. Never a new sign (signs are Keys, D-013).
- **Absence:** the app shows "where you were": the last place, the gate you were looking at, one unfinished record. Nothing has changed. Dust, not decay.
- **Rest day:** camp; a scene (the lamp; the rings; the sound of air from below); no step.
- The Custodian is never at camp while Dan is there (he met two readers in this hall, and the lamp was lit before Dan came). He is never behind Dan. He is always further in, until the doors.
- The ending is never gated on how many sealed things Dan has opened. "Read all of it" is a plea in a record, not a completion condition.
