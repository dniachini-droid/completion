## Shape

The Site is a descent. It was cut by a people who lived in the dark and thought in touch and sound, so its halls are long, low and rounded at the edges like the inside of a shell, and every surface within reach of a hand is cut with script. Water is rare and still. Air is dry and moves slowly from below. Nothing rots. The lamps are cut into the walls (a fork-in-a-cup every few paces) and wake in sequence when LIGHT is cut nearby, so a lit hall reads as a line of small flames going away round a corner.

Human intrusions come in from three sides at three depths: the salt crack (high, north), the road tunnel (high, south), the railway blast (low, east), and the ventilation shaft (the Linguist's way, top). Dan enters by the shaft.

## Gate rules (D-013, made concrete)

- **Word-doors** (a lintel with a rod-shaped blank): need a word. They show which by the signs already cut beside the blank.
- **Counting doors** (a tally that fills, and the RETURN mark): need a Key. In fiction they count returns from the surface. They show a tally with empty strokes.
- **Great doors** (both): the largest gates, one per region boundary from region 3 down.
- **Sealed niches and records** (a small counting lock): a Key each. These absorb extra Keys on strong weeks and are how up to ~250 sealed things a year are supplied without inventing story: each holds a record, an object, or a chorus fragment, all authored against `LIVES.md` and `CHARACTERS.md`.
- **Falls and blocks**: need MOVE-STONE or HOLD (a word).
- A gate that lacks what it needs simply waits. Nothing is ever locked *behind* Dan.

## Forks (the small choices, D-010)

At a fork the app names what each way is *like*, never what it holds: "the way with the cord dropped on it" / "the way the lamps are colder". Each way leads to a different life's records first; both are eventually reachable. Forks never gate story order: the authored sign order (SCRIPT §6) is the same whichever way is taken.

## Camp, absence, and the rules of the place

- **Camp** is the Lamp Hall, by the lamp, for the whole year (the evening close). Bedtime by the chosen time → something at camp in the morning: a line of an old record newly readable, a mark on the map, or a chorus fragment. Never a new sign (signs are Keys, D-013).
- **Absence:** the app shows "where you were": the last place, the gate you were looking at, one unfinished record. Nothing has changed. Dust, not decay.
- **Rest day:** camp; a scene (the lamp; the rings; the sound of air from below); no step.
- The Custodian is never at camp. He is never behind Dan. He is always further in.
