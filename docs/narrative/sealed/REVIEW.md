All other Key-sealed things are counting *locks*, not passages.
- *The rings reveal was not a reveal* (Dan knows a ring is a name from week 2). **Fixed:** the final reveal is the list: the five readers' personal names, known all year only by trade (act-first grammar: records never cut a name), every name ever given to him, and the empty ring at the end for the sixth reader, whose name he was never given; his own ring, KEEP-ONE, is a job where a name should be (the alternative, an empty ring for himself, was rejected because a player would read any empty ring as his own). - *Months 10–12 were nearly empty.* **Fixed:** the finest cut (month 11): the Builders read by touch; the Seed's smooth surface is dense text legible only by a lamp; no Builder read it; it says the message was never a question. - *The Seed promised a choice the mechanics denied.* **Fixed:** two blanks; the answering word cut there locks and does not carry (QUIET was cast to enter); the Custodian says so; the ending is still the binding.

**Cuttable threads (kept or wired):** the Copyist is now load-bearing (her gold on the fork-over-sign is why the Engineer read LOUD as a rating); filler signs in months 9–10 were cut; the second lamp stays (the Salt-Cutter's return).
