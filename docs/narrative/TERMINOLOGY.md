## Spelling and style

- Marks are written in SMALL CAPITALS in design docs (LAMP, FIRE); the app shows the mark itself and Dan's guess.
- Words are written with a hyphen between their marks in design docs (FIRE-GIVE). The app shows the marks locked together.
- Ages are never named or dated. Objects, trades and units carry the era.
- The app's voice never uses fantasy-speak: no "the Ancients", no capitalised abstractions, no ellipses for portent.
