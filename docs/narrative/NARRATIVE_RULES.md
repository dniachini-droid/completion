# Narrative Rules

> Binding rules for all narrative work. Expanded in Phase 3.

1. Know the truth before planting clues (`sealed/WORLD_TRUTH.md` first).
2. Keep layers separate: world truth · player knowledge · character beliefs · public history · false information · unresolved questions.
3. Track every important clue in `sealed/CLUE_LEDGER.md`.
4. Never casually retcon. Procedure: identify conflict → explain why → propose options → update affected docs → record in `DECISIONS.md`.
5. Respect the player's intelligence; don't over-explain; allow long uncertainty.
6. NPCs have their own desires and imperfect knowledge; they may distrust or oppose the player. No constant praise.
7. No generic fantasy, no generic AI prose. Prefer 20 meaningful things to 2,000 meaningless ones.
8. Any narrative specialist agent must read MASTER_BRIEF, PLAYER_MODEL, DESIGN_PRINCIPLES, GAME_DESIGN, CORE_LOOPS and this file first, and may not silently override decisions.
9. **Sealed vs open (D-015).** Dan is the only player and has chosen not to know the answers. Anything that reveals truth, twists, true identities, the ending or clue placement goes in `sealed/`. Open docs (`GAME_BIBLE.md`, `PITCHES.md`, `LOCATIONS.md`, `TERMINOLOGY.md`, research notes) describe only what a player could know from the box and the first hour. Chat, commit messages and PR text stay spoiler-free.
10. **The story goes somewhere (D-015).** Before any chapter or clue: the ending is fixed, the central question is set, and every major reveal is on a revelation map that builds towards that ending. Every thread must connect to the centre ("everything was related"); no filler mysteries, no reveals that go nowhere, no "we'll figure it out later". Each arc changes what Dan believes about what came before.
