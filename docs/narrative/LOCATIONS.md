# Locations — player-safe

The full geography, gates and what each needs are sealed (`sealed/SITE.md`, D-015). Visual notes are flavour only; art direction is Phase 4.

_Written 2026-09-23. Working names provisional (D-016)._

## The shape of the place

A descent. Long low halls rounded at the edges like the inside of a shell. Every surface within reach of a hand is cut with the script. Lamps are cut into the walls every few paces (a fork in a cup) and wake in sequence when the right word is cut nearby, so a lit hall reads as a line of small flames going away round a corner. Water is rare and still. The air is dry and moves slowly from below. Nothing rots. Nothing has changed since the last person was here.

## The first region (the first playable)

**The Mouth.** A capped ventilation shaft from the railway age, its padlock on the ground, cut clean. Two notices on the cap, one old and one new. Eleven metres of ladder. Then a dry passage cut square, and not by the railway.

**The Lamp Hall.** A long low hall. On a ledge, a clay lamp, lit. It has been lit since before you came. On the wall at eye height, a lesson someone left: a carved lamp beside a mark, a carved flame beside a mark, a word, and a line you can't read yet. Rings cut on every surface. On the side wall, a lintel with two marks beside a rod-shaped blank, and only stone beneath it. At the far end, a great door with a count that has not filled and a blank that wants a word. **Camp is here**, by the lamp.

**The Salt Gallery.** A gallery where the hill's rock salt meets the cut stone. The salt face is split from the top to about knee height, and the split has been sealed from the *other* side with stones and salt. Along the wall, a long tally in one hand, and above it a single ring on its own. A salt-pick in a niche.

**The Survey Cut.** Someone's camp: a cot, a small stove, a box of dead tapes, a printed page pinned to the rock, a notebook, and a shelf with a rod on it and a pencilled line underneath.

**The lintel on the side wall.** When the right word is cut, the lamps wake down the hall and the stone under the lintel opens.

**The head of the stair.** Beyond the lintel: a landing, a niche with a count, and the top of a long stair going down. Somewhere on the second flight, a door someone once called open.

## Named by shape only (later regions)

The stair. The water. A reading room. A room that still smells of powder. The makers' halls. A landing where the corridor turns. Three great doors. The deep. The bottom.
