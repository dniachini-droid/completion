The full geography, gates and what each needs are sealed (`sealed/SITE.md`, D-015). Visual notes are flavour only; art direction is Phase 4.

_Written 2026-09-23. Working names provisional (D-016)._

## The shape of the place

A descent. Long high halls rounded at the edges like the inside of a shell, and low side passages. Every surface within reach of a hand is cut with the script. Lamps are cut into the walls every few paces (a fork in a cup) and wake in sequence when the right word is cut nearby, so a lit hall reads as a line of small flames going away round a corner. Water is rare and still. The air is dry and moves slowly from below. Nothing rots. Nothing has changed since the last person was here.

## The first region (the first playable)

**The Mouth.** A capped ventilation shaft from the railway age, its padlock on the ground, cut clean. Two notices on the cap, one old and one new. Eleven metres of ladder. Then a dry passage cut true, and not by the railway.

**The Lamp Hall.** A long high hall. On a ledge, a clay lamp, lit. It was lit when you came down, and there is no oil in it. Along both walls at head height, cups cut into the stone, one every few paces, empty. On the wall at eye height, a short wall that teaches: a carved lamp beside a mark, a carved flame beside a mark, a word, and a line you can't read yet. Rings cut on every surface. On the side wall, a lintel with two marks beside a rod-shaped blank, and only stone beneath it. At the far end, a great door with a count that has not filled and a blank that wants a word. **Camp is here**, by the lamp.

**The Salt Gallery.** A gallery where the hill's rock salt meets the cut stone. The salt face is split from the top to about knee height, and the split has been sealed from the *other* side with stones and salt. Along the wall, a long tally in one hand, and above it a single ring on its own. A salt-pick in a niche.

**The Survey Cut.** Someone's camp: a cot with a pair of boots under it, laced and dry; on the cot, a notebook with a pencil in it and a tin box with a row of cut strokes on its lid; on a shelf, a rod of stone the length of a forearm, one edge finer than a knife. More of the camp comes to light as you come back.

**The lintel on the side wall.** When the right word is cut, the lamps wake down the hall and the stone under the lintel opens.

**The head of the stair.** Beyond the lintel: a landing, a niche with a count, and the top of a long stair going down. On the second flight, a small door with a count, shut, with a finger's width of dark down one edge; beside it, one ring cut sharper than the rest; under the second turn, a recess.

## Named by shape only (later regions)

The stair. The water. A reading room. A room that still smells of powder. The makers' halls. A landing where the corridor turns. Three great doors. The deep. The bottom.
