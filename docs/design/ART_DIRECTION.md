### Early reading (Claude's, hypothesis only)
- Nearly everything got a yes, so the lists alone discriminate weakly. The strongest signal is in the "loves" and the one "no".
- **Loves** cluster in two places: *precise, clean, glowing-on-dark interfaces* (Destiny's menus, Mass Effect's map) and *clean impossible geometry* (Monument Valley); and *ancient-machine / strange-beautiful* (Stargate, Annihilation), grounded by *worn and practical* (Andor).
- The one **no** (Outer Wilds) is the most handmade, warm and cosy of the list. With "cold stone", that points away from warm-and-rustic and towards cool, precise, graphic.
- The rest of the session (light, line, type, map, motion) uses forced choices between visible samples to sharpen this.
