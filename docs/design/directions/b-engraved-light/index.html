Direction B

Engraved Light

An ancient instrument you are learning to operate: dark basalt-blue stone, and cold light cut into it in fine, exact lines.

Warm is rare: only the clay lamp and the one thing to press.

Carved Cinzel for titles and buttons; a quiet book serif, EB Garamond, for every sentence.

A strict grid of framed plates with corner ticks; the world sits in a viewport, like looking through the instrument.

Smooth and exact every day. At the big moments, the light runs along every line at once.
