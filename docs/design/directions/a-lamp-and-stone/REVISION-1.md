# Direction A: revision 1

This answers `CRITIQUE-1.md`. Every blocker and every should-fix is addressed, plus most of the nits. Screens were checked at 390×844 and 360×780.

## Across all screens
- **Layout that holds on smaller phones.** The screen now fills the viewport (`100svh`, up to 440 px wide). The interface is laid out in normal flow: the header at the top, and the day, buttons and nav anchored to the bottom. The painting is cropped rather than squashed. The few words placed on the painting (the morning's "Ahead", the map labels, the delve ring) are positioned in % of the screen, so they stay with what they point at. Nothing is clipped at 360×780.
- **The warm button is no longer a glossy pill.** It is now carved capitals sitting in a soft pool of lamplight, with no hard edge, over a thin slot lit from within. It keeps a 60 px hit area and the breathing swell.
- **Type.** Cinzel capitals are kept for place names and small labels (at least 13 px, weight 600). Jobs, list titles, record titles, the arrival line, the nav and the Low/Normal/High words are now in Marcellus, in sentence case, at 16–28 px. That is still carved letters everywhere.
- **Cold light in the place.** Every hall view now has a luminous cold far end, and the far door has a cold halo. The Cut is cut on the walls and glows faintly cold, cold dust drifts, and there is a cold rim on the lintel. The salt face is lit cold from below. The warm light (the lamp, the one warm button, done jobs) now shows up against real cold light rather than against black.
- **Flow between screens.** Morning Begin opens a delve on *Order the cat's medication* (`delve.html?job=cat` swaps the title). The Course row opens the Course delve. The delve's end is a breather, with "Next delve" and "Done for now"; in the prototype you reach it by tapping the ring. On the map, the lintel opens the cut, the Salt Gallery opens the arrival, and the Lamp Hall opens Today. The arrival's "Done for now" returns to Today, and "Read the wall" opens the record, whose back link reads "‹ The Salt Gallery".

## Per screen
- **Lamp Hall (morning, cut, camp).** Repainted as **one continuous shell-like vault**: 44 blended depth slices, each lit by cold from the far end and warm from the lamp side, with courses and joints in the stone instead of repeated ribs. The cross-sections tighten a little with distance. The wall-cups are recesses cut into the wall, with a lit lip and inner shadow, at irregular spacing. They stay dark except in the cut. The lintel sits on the wall in true perspective above a blind doorway, and its blank is a recess rather than a dashed outline. The lamp stands on a shelf clear of the text block.
- **Morning.** Low / normal / high are Marcellus lower case at 17 px, with a warm point under "normal". "from last night's bedtime" sits on its own line beneath. The header wraps if it needs to, and Map is now the plain word. The job is in sentence case, and the teaser uses `text-wrap: pretty`.
- **Delve.** The ring is now a full circle: a visible cold track (about 34%, with haze) and the warm fill with its bead. The passage behind is darkened and softened inside the ring's radius, so the ring owns the centre. The title is "Course", with "The first of two delves." beneath. A breather state was added.
- **Map.** Sub-labels appear only for "you are here" and the sealed things. Every place is a target at least 44 px tall. The faint "further down" label was removed; the stair's shape speaks for itself. The labels are placed in %.
- **Record.** The marks are now 48 px buttons with the guesses beneath. The first unguessed mark breathes warm, and its candidate meanings are open underneath as warm, lit words ("rope · bread · morning"). Tapping any mark shows its candidates; tapping a candidate sets the guess, with a question mark. "Done for now" is now quiet cold text. The orphan ring is gone, the neighbouring rows are at about half opacity, and the line sits inside the 24 px gutter.
- **Cut.** The close view now fills the width with the lintel. The blank is a rod-shaped recess, and the marks are cut as dark grooves that then fill with warm light from inside. Beneath the lintel is coursed stone, not fog. **Dan has one input:** a warm "Cut the word" button. The hall pulls back and the cups wake one pair at a time as soft glows in their recesses. The opening now shows a stair going down, lit by the lamp behind you, with cold air below. For speed, the sequence runs on light overlay layers above a static hall.
- **Complete.** The salt breaks through a wall of large cut blocks, with no arched frame and a soft, broken edge. It is crystalline, dark at the top and lit cold from below. The crack is a dark split that narrows to nothing at knee height, packed with dark rubble. The arrival line is in Marcellus. The sequence (fade up, pull back, the salt waking, four cairn stones settling, the words) now starts only after the first frame has painted.
- **Camp.** The lamp has clay grain, a chip at the rim and soot at the nozzle. The shelf is seated in a recess cut into the wall. The hall behind is the new vault.
- **Satchel.** The tick controls are now a visible 14 px cold ring that becomes a warm point when ticked. The satchel is worn leather with a flap, a buckle strap and a long strap hung from a peg. The finds are the player-safe tin box with cut strokes on its lid, and the stone rod.

## Headless timing (the reviewer saw the big moments already settled)
- Both sequences now start from the first painted frame, not from page load. The cut waits for the "Cut the word" press. In this prototype it also starts by itself 1.2 s after the first paint, so a screenshot or a passive viewer still sees it. At 1500 ms the cut shows its opening state (the empty recess and the button), at about 2.5 s it is mid-cut, and at 9 s it has settled. The arrival is mid-sequence at 1500 ms: dimmed, with no cairn yet.
- Animations now change only light layers: the lit cups, the opening, the cairn and the dimming are separate from the heavy painted SVGs, so frames stay cheap.
