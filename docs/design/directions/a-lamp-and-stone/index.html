Direction A

Lamp and Stone

You, in a vast cold hall, holding the only warm thing. Cold is the place; warm is you and what you’ve earned.

Each screen is a painted view of a place, with a few carved words in its darkest part. Carved letters set everything. No cards, no boxes, no lines of light: soft points, haze and one warm thing to press.

  1. The morningThe Lamp Hall, the next job, and one warm button.
  2. A delveA warm ring slowly fills around the time left while the light moves down the passage.
  3. The mapPlaces like lamps in the dark: warm where you’ve been, cold where you’ve only seen.
  4. A recordThe long tally. Cold marks in the stone, your guesses beneath them in warm.
  5. Cutting a wordThe big moment. Two marks cut, the word locks, and the lamps wake down the hall.
  6. Day completeArriving at the Salt Gallery. A cairn is stacked. “That’s the day. Enough.”
  7. CampThe evening close, by the lamp, with bedtime set for 23:00.
  8. The satchelA list you asked for, below niches lit from beneath. No counts.
  9. The daybookWeek two, written for you, under the ladder you came down.

Nine static screens, drawn for a 390 × 844 phone. The two big moments (cutting a word, day complete) play once as you open them.
