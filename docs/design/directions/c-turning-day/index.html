Direction C

The Turning Day

The glow follows the day. Morning and delves are cold blue-violet; the arrival turns everything gold; camp is fully warm. Warm light is what a finished day earns.

Two voices: the place speaks in carved letters (the Lamp Hall, the marks, the records); your own life speaks in plain print (jobs, times, buttons, the satchel, the daybook).

Full-bleed paintings of stone half lost in luminous fog, and one card of light carrying the one thing to do.

  1. 01
    The morning
    Cold, clear light in the Lamp Hall; one card carries the next job.
  2. 02
    A delve
    A soft ring of cold light fills as the minutes pass; the passage keeps moving.
  3. 03
    The map
    The first region from above, through drifting fog that thins where you have walked.
  4. 04
    A record
    One line of the long tally floats across the fog, your guesses beneath it.
  5. 05
    Cutting a word
    Two marks cut in order, the word locks, and the lamps wake down the hall.
  6. 06
    Day complete
    The Salt Gallery: the whole screen turns from cold to gold. Enough.
  7. 07
    Camp
    Fully warm now. The lamp, the bedtime, and what waits in the morning.
  8. 08
    The satchel
    A list, on request, in plain print. Nothing counted.
  9. 09
    The daybook
    Week 2 on warm paper, written for you in plain English.
