Direction C

The Turning Day

The stone is always lit violet. The day turns through light inside the scene: a trace of amber at dawn, the floor lit by mid-afternoon, the salt face gold at the arrival, the lower half of the hall glowing at camp. Warm light is what a finished day earns.

Two voices: the place speaks in carved capitals (the Lamp Hall, the marks, the records); your own life speaks in plain print, sentence case (jobs, times, buttons, the satchel, the daybook).

Close, low views of cut stone in drifting fog, and one bank of light carrying the one thing to do.

  1. 01
    The morning
    Violet light along the Lamp Hall; the next job in a bank of light. Satchel, daybook and camp are one quiet tap below.
  2. 02
    The same day, afternoon
    Two jobs done: amber lamplight now claims the floor.
  3. 03
    The same day, done
    Nothing next: the day is enough, and camp is by the lamp.
  4. 04
    A delve
    A soft ring of light fills as the minutes pass; the passage keeps flowing. It ends in a breather.
  5. 05
    The map
    The first region from above, through drifting fog that thins where you have walked.
  6. 06
    A record
    One line of the long tally, large; the one new mark glows to be guessed.
  7. 07
    Cutting a word
    The rod cuts two marks into the lintel, the word locks, the lamps wake down the hall.
  8. 08
    Day complete
    The Salt Gallery: gold light rises up the crystal face. Enough.
  9. 09
    Camp
    Lamplight fills the lower half of the hall; only the vault stays violet.
  10. 10
    The satchel
    A list, on request, in plain print. Nothing counted.
  11. 11
    The daybook
    Week 2, lamp-lit words on a stone wall, written for you in plain English.
