Prompt 7

The map

The first region (the way in, the first hall, two side galleries, a door, the head of a stair), shown five ways.

ASection drawing

The hill cut open from the side, like a surveyor's drawing. Depth reads as going down.

BTop-down plan

An architect's floor plan. Clear, orderly, easy to read at a glance.

CA scene you look into

You see where you are, in the first person. The map is the place itself.

DA single path

One route you walk down. Only the next step matters; the rest is dotted.

EGlowing node map

Places as lit points on dark, like Mass Effect's galaxy map.
