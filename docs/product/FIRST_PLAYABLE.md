## What it is for
One question (rule 14): **does wanting to go further in make Dan start real things?** Everything below either helps answer it or makes the answer honest. Small, complete and beautiful (D-004, rule 13).

## In

**The day**
- Today (the morning screen): capacity (Low / Normal / High, suggested from bedtime, one tap), **one** next job leading, today's other jobs beneath, Swap, "I can't start" (a teaser from just ahead, then one tiny step, then "10 minutes?"). Only today's jobs; never a backlog (P7).
- Day sizes, the day's edge, opening late and "Keep going" after day complete, as in `BALANCING.md` §6.
- Day complete: the arrival (a named place, or a camp with a view carrying one thing to look at), with success locked in and rest as the main offer.
- Life happens: an appointment added afterwards counts as a main job.

**Doing a job**
- The delve: the dial (25 / 30 / 45 / 60), runs of delves on one route line, the glowing ring, breathers that end by themselves, Step away, Finish here, Start it now (D-033, D-036, D-037).
- **Enough, then more:** the Course's own enough moment, then Continue or Back to today (D-047).
- Jobs without a timer: Begin, go, Done on return.
- Any job can be a delve, Dan's choice, switchable for today (D-041).

**Planning and lists**
- The week planner, as `PLANNER.md` → First playable: rhythms (N a week, set days, every 2 weeks; optional length, enough and time), This week, Plan my week, tap to move / set a time / remove, add a one-off or appointment, Today from the plan with capacity on top, the forecast line, waypoints on the map, the Sunday offer.
- The satchel: one line or many; the main-job rule; *someday* after about 3 weeks; dated items and the once-a-day passed-date question.
- Editing jobs and rhythms on request only; **preloaded with Dan's own set** (D-043 F8).

**The world**
- One region: the way in, the first hall, two side galleries, one door in view from the start, the head of a stair (`narrative/GAME_BIBLE.md`), with its side passages, side chambers and open route (`BALANCING.md` §5).
- The map at three zoom levels, opening on where Dan is; walked routes, "you are here", forecast waypoints.
- Two lives' records, fully written; the first marks; decoding; **the first word in week 2–3** and the cutting cinematic; the stair's "Go down" (D-039).
- Keys: 5 useful a week, the floor of 2, opening the moment they're earned (`BALANCING.md` §3).
- Finds: avoided jobs, switching after a long stretch, side chambers, camp.
- High days: the deep push; partial signs only where written.

**Evening and the week**
- Camp: the wind-down, bedtime tapped, something waiting in the morning if bedtime was kept.
- The trail with its first relic (about the 7th marker).
- The daybook (the Chronicle) as the week close: what was done, up to 3 things learned, the glimpse ahead, and the month's "so far" (`BALANCING.md` §7).
- Absence: "where you were" after one small real job; the first day back suggested Low.

**Always on (from the principles)**
No shame, no debt, no counts of what's left, no red; failure is information (rules 7–9, UX 6). The world visibly moves (D-041).

## The test
- **5–6 weeks** of normal use; not judged on week 4 alone (D-023).
- **The app notes by itself** (no admin): which jobs were started and when; whether avoided jobs got done more often; "I can't start" use and whether a job followed; returns after low days and gaps; planned, moved and done (kept internally, never shown).
- **Then a short chat:** did wanting to go further in make you start real things? What felt like a chore? Do you remember what you've learned down there, and does it feel like discovery or homework (D-035)? Did the week planner help, or feel like admin?
- **After 3–4 weeks of play:** the language pass (D-046).
