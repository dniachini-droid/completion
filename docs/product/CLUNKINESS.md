# The clunkiness pass (2026-09-25, D-090)

Dan: "the app is extremely clunky. It's pretty but doesn't function well." Every daily path was walked at phone size
(440 × 956 and 360 × 780), each tap counted, and anything Dan would never miss was cut. Spoiler-free.

## Taps, before → after

| What Dan does | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Add a one-off job to Friday (from the Week) | Add → scroll down to the box → type → Fri → Add: **3 taps + a scroll** | **+** on Friday → type → Add (or the keyboard's Done): **2 taps**, no scroll, no day to pick |
| Move a job to another day | the job → the day → Save: **3** | the job → the day: **2** (it moves at once) |
| Give a job a time | the job → Set a time → the time → wheel → Save: **4 + wheel** | the job → the time → wheel: **2 + wheel** (kept as soon as it's set) |
| Take a time off a job | the job → Any time → Save: **3** | the job → Any time: **2** |
| Change bedtime at camp | Change → the time (or − / +) → Set: **3 + wheel** | the time → wheel: **1 + wheel** |
| Go to bed | Camp (a link on Today all day, even at noon, when it could only say "come back later") → Go to sleep | In the evening only: **To camp, for the night** at the end of Today's list (or the gold To camp once the day's work is done) → Go to sleep. Same 2 taps; Camp never shows when it can't be used |
| Switch Records ⇄ Marks | the screen rebuilt: tabs, heading and painting faded and rose again | swaps in place: nothing moves |

## What else changed
- **Today opens on the next job.** The story "Ahead" was often a dozen lines and pushed the day's work down; it now shows
  four lines, and a tap on it reads the rest (a tap again folds it).
- **Today's foot** is Satchel · Week · Daybook. Camp left it (above). The trial controls moved from camp to the Daybook
  (top right, "Trial").
- **The Week** lists every day still ahead, even an empty one, so there is always somewhere to add to. "Not now" beside
  Plan my week is gone (the arrow does it). The day and time controls are the same size as before; there is no Save.
- **The phone warming during a delve** (Dan). Found three causes, all fixed without changing the look:
  1. the delve's dust and ring were redrawn at the screen's full rate (60–120 times a second on a newer iPhone): now 30,
     as the paintings' own live layers already were;
  2. the dust layer covered the whole screen at double resolution (about 7 MB sent to the graphics chip every frame):
     now at the screen's own size, a quarter of that (the dust is soft points of light and looks the same);
  3. the painting behind the blurred screens (Week, Choose, Satchel, Records, Marks, Daybook, "I can't start") kept
     moving under the blur, so the phone re-blurred the whole screen 30 times a second for nothing: it is now drawn once
     and held.
  Locking the phone during a delve still stops all drawing; the alert still rings at the end.

## Walked and kept as they are
- **The morning**: one screen, one button (On to today).
- **Doing a job**: Delve (a short job starts at once; a longer one opens the dial set to its enough → Begin) → the
  delve → its end → Back to today. A job done away from the phone: Begin → Done → its step → Back to today. Each tap
  is a real moment; none was a question Dan had to answer twice.
- **Something else…** (choose a delve), **the satchel**, **I can't start**: already short.
