_Status: **approved by Dan** (2026-09-24, D-055), with his four answers (D-054)._

## In one breath
The MVP is the first playable almost whole. Phase 5 already cut the features hard; what is left is the loop itself and what Dan meets in the test's six weeks. **The real size of the build is in paintings and words, not features**, so that is where this page budgets. The test asks one thing: does wanting to go further in make Dan **start** real things, especially the ones he avoids?

## What "true" means (the bar for every item)
An item is in the MVP if **any** answer is yes:
1. Without it, is the core loop broken, or the test dishonest?
2. Without it, would a day feel like a task manager with a picture?
3. Without it, would normal use hit a wall within six weeks (a gap, a bad week, a job that changes)?
4. Does Dan meet it before the test ends (the first word in week 2–3, the week close, a gap, the month's "so far")?

Everything else is cut or made simpler. A second way into something that already has one is the first thing cut.

## The heart (never cut)
The thin, complete slice (MASTER_BRIEF §66), exactly as `CONCEPT.md` §2 plays it:
**open → one job, obvious → Begin → real action → back → the step plays → day complete → an arrival → a line of a life → a sealed thing ahead you can see → want the next real action.**
If a later cut ever threatens one link in this chain, the cut loses.

# The central test

**The question (rule 14, §61):** *does wanting to progress the world make Dan more likely to **initiate** meaningful real-world actions?*

- **Meaningful** = the jobs on Dan's own list, above all the avoided ones: admin, Spanish, housework, costly one-offs (`PLAYER_MODEL.md`).
- **Initiate** = starting. Not total hours, not Keys, not places reached. The gym already happens some weeks; the Course is enjoyed. The test is sharpest where starting is hard.
- **The competitor** is the couch, the phone and YouTube (`PLAYER_MODEL.md`), not another app.

## Before day 1: the baseline (10 minutes, a chat)
Dan describes a normal recent fortnight in rough words: how often the gym, Spanish, the Course, admin and housework actually happened, and how the couch moment usually goes. Claude writes it in `docs/PLAYTEST_LOG.md`. Memory is rough; it gives a direction, not a number to beat.
Claude also writes down, **before** the test, what a yes and a no would look like (below), so the answer can't be bent afterwards.

## What the app notes by itself
On the phone only, never shown to Dan during play (UX 6, D-045), and no work for him:
- **Each job:** when it was started, and **how**: from the app (Begin, a delve) or recorded afterwards (Done with no Begin). This is the test's sharpest line: a game that pulls starts vs a logbook.
- Whether it was an **avoided** job; swaps.
- **"I can't start":** each use, and whether a job began within 30 minutes.
- **Days:** opened, complete, capacity chosen; gaps of 3+ days and the return after them. There are **no notifications**, so every return is Dan's own.
- **The world:** whether arrivals and records were opened or skipped past; which sealed thing Dan looked at.
- **The plan:** planned, moved, done (D-045).

**Not collected:** mood, health, anything typed into a job or list beyond what the app needs, location, contacts, time spent in other apps. No analytics service, nothing sent anywhere. At the end, the app shows a plain summary on the phone; **Dan decides** whether to share it with Claude.

## Talking (three short chats, never homework)
- **End of week 1** (5 minutes): anything broken, confusing or annoying? Bugs only; no questions about motivation, so the test isn't steered.
- **Week 6** (the main chat, 20 minutes), from `FIRST_PLAYABLE.md`: did wanting to go further in make you start real things? Can you remember one moment it did? What felt like a chore? What do you remember learning down there, and is it discovery or homework (D-035)? Did the planner help or feel like admin? When did you *not* open it, and what happened instead?
- **Any time Dan likes:** a sentence about a moment he noticed ("went to the gym to see the door"). Never asked for, never counted.
Chores are asked about **before** favourites, so politeness doesn't bury the problems.

## Evidence for (the world is doing the work)
- Dan names **specific moments** where the Quiet pulled him into starting: a door he wanted open, a record he wanted to finish, the next place.
- **Avoided jobs start more often** than in the baseline, and they start from the app (Begin), not only logged afterwards.
- **"I can't start" is followed by a job** more often than not.
- He **comes back after gaps and low days** without being reminded.
- Use **holds or recovers through weeks 4–6**, past the novelty dip.
- He remembers what he learned, and talks about the Quiet between sessions (questions, guesses, "what's behind that door?").
- Low days get completed instead of becoming zero days.

## Evidence against (or: it's something else doing the work)
- Jobs mostly **logged afterwards**: the app is a record of a life that would have happened anyway.
- The gym and the Course rise; **avoided jobs don't**.
- Arrivals and records are **skipped past**; the steps are something to get through.
- He credits **the planner or the list**, not the world ("it was the schedule"). Then the tools work and the game doesn't, yet.
- Use drops in weeks 3–5 and **doesn't recover**.
- "I can't start" is used and **nothing follows**.
- Anything feels like an **obligation or a debt**, or he opens it out of guilt (against rules 9 and 11, whatever else the numbers say).
- Keys, places or story feel like homework, or can't be remembered.

## What not to overinterpret
- **Week 1** (novelty) and **week 4** (the usual dip), alone.
- **One bad week.** Life, illness and hard days happen; the design expects them (rule 9). Look at the six weeks.
- **Total hours, steps, Keys, places.** More isn't the question; starting is.
- **Planner adherence.** The plan is a forecast (D-048); a moved plan is not a failure.
- **Politeness.** Dan is also the client; his "it's great" counts less than a remembered moment.
- **One player, no control group.** The answer is a strong or weak signal for Dan, not proof. That is enough to decide what to build next.
- **Health.** The test is not a measure of how Dan is doing, and nothing in it is clinical. It complements his psychologist's advice; it is not treatment.

## Reading the answer, and what follows
| Reading | Looks like | Next |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes** | Several named moments; avoided jobs up and started from the app; returns after gaps; holds past week 4 | Personal alpha (Phase 10): keep playing, tune the numbers, the language pass (D-046), grow content |
| **Mixed** | Jobs up, but credited to the tools; or strong early, fading later | Find the cause, not a feature (MASTER_BRIEF §67–70): story too slow or too thin? rewards unreadable? friction? too much to read? |
| **No** | Logged afterwards, skipped past, nothing changes after novelty | The core bet fails. Revisit the concept with Dan; don't pile on mechanics (rule 12) |
