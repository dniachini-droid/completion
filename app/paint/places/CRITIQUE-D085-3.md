**pt-b-3.A, the lintel (sealed): PASS.** The rod-shaped blank (u .54–.71, v .31–.32) is the brightest value, a clean-edged recess the length of a rod, and the two marks sit left of it; the slab on its posts reads as a lintel over a blocked doorway. The painter's worry (slab like a plate) does not produce a wrong reading of the look-at. Should fix: the blank's floor is lit so evenly it could pass for a light slot; darken its lower inner edge a touch so it reads as a recess, not a lit opening.

**pt-b-3.B, the head of the Stair: PASS.** The second lamp (u .17–.30, v .43–.46) reads at once as a small clay oil lamp in a niche, and with no flame it reads unlit. The lighting from the front (not from below, as the brief asks) is a departure from the brief, not a wrong reading. Should fix: the pale halo round the niche's mouth could suggest light inside; soften it.

**pt-b-3.C, the top flight: PASS.** The worn top of the rail at the turn (u .45–.66, v .49) is a lit line continuous with the rail cut along the left wall, so it reads as a handrail catching light. Small and far, but found. The steps do not look knee-high, which is not a wrong reading of the look-at. Should fix: the warm pool on the wall just above the streak makes it lean toward a wall light; move that glow below the rail (lamps are below) and bring the camera a step nearer the turn.

**pt-b-4.A, the Day 9 sheet: PASS.** The count (u .56–.63, v .47–.52) is the lit focus, on a pale mass that the tally rail stops at and resumes past, so "the tally goes under this" reads. The crust is a smooth bulge rather than crystalline salt (the painter's worry), but it reads as a white deposit over the wall, not as a separate object. Should fix: a few crystal glints and a rougher, whiter edge where it meets the rail.

**pt-pl-w4-hollow: FAIL (wrong reading).** The whole look-at reads as **an eye**: an almond-shaped mound with a glowing white arc along its top like a lit eyelid, the count's five curved strokes at its corner read as **eyelashes**, and the dark diagonal band across it as a lid crease. Inside, the four pressed places are small bright white ovals with ragged edges, so they read as **holes or spots of light** (or dice pips), not flat places pressed into salt. Smallest fix: remove the bright rim arc and the dark diagonal band; cut the count as straight short strokes on the rim, away from the silhouette's corner; make the four places matte, slightly lighter-than-surround flat facets with a soft sheen and a faint shadowed lip on one side (pressed in), no white core, no ragged edge, set in a loose irregular footprint layout, not a square.

**pt-b-4.B, the salt block: PASS.** The hook (u .44–.49, v .47–.50) is incised, lit on one flank, centred on the brightest block; it reads as a hook cut into salt, not as a pin or wire. Should fix (clue fidelity, not yet a wrong reading): the "dot" inside the closed hook is barely visible and the curl reads as a spiral; close the curl into a clean loop and cut a separate round pit inside it, large enough to see at phone size.

**pt-b-4.C, the Lower Door close: FAIL (wrong reading of a planted mark).** The look-at is fine: seven empty cut strokes (u .23–.54, v .48–.52) read as an empty count. But the mark under it, "a path doubling back" (u .22–.50, v .55–.59), reads as **a loose thread or wire lying on the door**: a thin raised line, a slack wave, and a small ring at one end like an eyelet. A clue must not read wrongly. Smallest fix: cut it as a groove with the same width and flank lighting as the count strokes; make it a firm hairpin (one run out, a tight turn, a near-parallel run back) with no slack wave; remove the ring at its end. Also watch: the bar-with-a-drop beside the blank (u .59, v .49–.52) is close to an exclamation mark; keep the drop joined to the bar as a teardrop swelling, not a separate round dot.

**pt-pl-w3-salt-lit: FAIL (unchanged wrong reading).** Re-baked unchanged, so the round-2 wrong reading stands: the split (u .37–.56, v .44–.55) is a rounded patch filled edge to edge with same-size glossy brown ovals, which reads as **a tray of eggs, beans or a sack**, not river stones packed in a natural split; and the peak is a warm hot-spot on bare salt above it (u .35–.50, v .42), which reads as a lamp.
