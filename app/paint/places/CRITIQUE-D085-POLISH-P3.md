**pt-b-6.A, the side passage: FAIL.** Better composed than before (round vault left with a warm lamp pool, a round-arched opening into the square gallery right), but the look-at, the clasp where square stone keys into round, is not found: the teeth along the jamb (u .45–.55, v .47–.63) are near the value of the dark gallery beyond and vanish at phone size; in close-up their chevron chisel texture reads as metal tread plate. Worse, across the top of the opening (u .60–1.0, v .25–.36) a pale row of vertical slats with a torn dark lower edge reads as a **fence/balustrade or broken slatted boards** hanging in the arch. The frame reads as "a doorway from a tunnel into a dark room", not "two ages' stone clasped". Fix: delete or darken the slatted band; lift the teeth to the lamp-lit value of the round wall so they bite visibly into the square side's shadow, and make them bigger (fewer, larger blocks).

**pt-pl-w6-square-gallery: KEEP-NEW.** Same composition, exposure lifted: walls, cracks and the flat ceiling's chisel marks now read, the corridor has more depth, and the broken-stone slope stays the only lit thing at the vanishing point. At least as good, slightly richer.

**pt-b-6.B, the crew's wall: KEEP-OLD.** The tablet and its rows of rings are unchanged (still reads as rows, a ledger). But the new version loses the warm light from the left that the brief asks for and adds a cold violet bloom at the right frame edge (u 1.0, v .35–.45) that pulls the eye off the tablet to the edge. Cooler and flatter than the old, not richer.

**pt-pl-w6-wall-shelf: PASS.** The towel-rail/strip-light reading is gone: the line is now flat and pale, no cylindrical shading, no bloom, with a short tick at each end (u .30–.57, v .495), and reads as a drawn/scratched level line along the shelf's lip. Should fix (not a wrong reading): the left tick hangs lower and thicker than the right, faintly like a bracket end; make the two ticks equal and centred on the line. The shelf itself is a dark slot and the frame is dark, which is acceptable.

**pt-pl-w6-folder: KEEP-NEW.** HILL on the spine is still legible and the folder is still the lit object. But the look-at itself got worse: the mug's body is warped (bulging, dented left wall) and its warm reflection is broken into two blotches with a smudgy dark band between them (u .45–.60, v .50–.56), reading as a dent or a smear rather than one clean glint; and the box has gone warmer brown, pushing it further toward leather, the old should-fix. **pt-cv-01, the ladder's foot: PASS.** The railing is gone. The ladder is now on the wall facing the camera, two straight rails with round rungs lit on their upper edges, the spacing shrinking toward the sky square. It reads at once as a ladder going up a shaft. The chalk "↑ 40 FT" sits beside it on the same wall, upright, with the arrow pointing along the ladder toward the daylight. Should fix (not a wrong reading): the strip at the bottom edge (v .94–1.0) is too dark to read as the shaft's floor, and the ladder's feet don't visibly rest on it.
