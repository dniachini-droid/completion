## 1. pt-b-2.B, the rod (6/10; was 5) — out (needs 7)

*A burnt plank on a ledge, its top face glowing.*
- Better: the rod crosses the frame at a low diagonal (u .17–.82) and is the brightest thing.
1. **The light is a broad warm smear along the top face** (u .30–.55 v .47–.51) plus a gold pool on the shelf under it, not "a thin line of light running along the edge". Narrow it to one line on the edge (`reach` ≈ rod width), and remove the pool.
2. **The rod reads as charred wood or driftwood**: a ragged, broken left end and a flat plank section. The marks on the handle read as ruler ticks, evenly spaced along the edge (u .45–.60 v .50). Round the section, taper the tip to a point, cluster the marks on the handle, show the hook.
3. **The upper 45 % is empty murk** with a warm-brown smudge (u .40–.60 v .10–.25). The pencilled line (v .60) is there but reads as a stray scratch.

**What is still short:** dark fine stone that reads as a made thing, one hairline of gold along its edge, a dark calm wall behind.
